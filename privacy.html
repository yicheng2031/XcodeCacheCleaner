关于隐私About privacy

最后更新：2026-08-11Last updated: 2026-08-11

不收集数据No data collection

Xcode Cache Cleaner 不收集、上传、出售或分享任何个人数据。应用不需要账号或登录，也不包含分析统计、广告追踪或云同步。Xcode Cache Cleaner does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It requires no account or login and includes no analytics, advertising tracking, or cloud sync.

所有操作都在本机完成Everything happens on-device

扫描结果、分类选择和删除操作都只在你的 Mac 上处理。应用不会把文件名、项目内容、磁盘信息或扫描结果发送到服务器。Scanning, category selection, and deletion are processed on your Mac. The app does not send file names, project contents, disk information, or scan results to a server.

文件夹访问权限Folder access

如果你使用 Mac App Store 沙盒版本，应用可能请求访问你选择的文件夹，例如 ~/Library，以扫描和清理 Xcode 相关缓存。权限只用于本机操作，可以在 macOS 设置中撤销。The Mac App Store sandbox build may ask for access to folders you choose, such as ~/Library, to scan and clean Xcode-related caches. This permission is used only on-device and can be revoked in macOS settings.
